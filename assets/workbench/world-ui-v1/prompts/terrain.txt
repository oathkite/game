Production texture for a friendly pixel-art frog tank artillery game. A seamless repeating square cross-section of warm clay soil with mossy grey stone chips and a few tiny copper mineral veins, inspired by the attached tank's terracotta, cream and dark blue-grey outlines. Strict chunky pixel art, visible square pixel clusters, 16-color palette, no soft brushwork, no gradients. Entire square filled with dirt material, evenly detailed, no sky, no grassy top edge, no objects, no tank, no text. This will be clipped by a destructible collision mask at runtime. The edges must tile horizontally and vertically. Low contrast so shells and tank silhouettes read clearly.
